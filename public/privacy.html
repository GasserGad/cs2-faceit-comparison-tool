CS2 H2H

Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026.

CS2 H2H is built to collect as little as possible. There are no accounts, no cookies, no advertising and no analytics or tracking scripts.

What we process

  • The nicknames you search. They are sent to the FACEIT Data API to look up public player data. Search results are kept in server memory for up to 10 minutes so repeat searches are faster, then discarded.
  • Your IP address. It is used in memory to enforce rate limits (to protect the Service from abuse) and is forgotten after about a minute. Our own logs do not record IP addresses or what you searched.
  • Basic request logs. Our logs record the time, page path, response status and duration of each request, to keep the Service running and fix errors.

Third parties

  • FACEIT receives the nicknames you search, under FACEIT's privacy policy.
  • Our hosting provider may keep its own network and access logs, including IP addresses, under its own privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone else.

Public player data

The statistics shown are public on faceit.com and come from FACEIT. To change or remove your FACEIT data, contact FACEIT. We do not store player profiles beyond the short in-memory cache described above.

Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
